Locked-screen test

This separate diagnostic page does not change your NPTE app. First test audio playback, then test speech recognition. This test cannot bypass iOS restrictions.

1. Background audio

Tap Play, then lock the screen. A short beep should sound every few seconds for about a minute. Notice whether it continues after locking.

2. Microphone

Unlock the phone. Tap Start listening, grant permission, then lock the phone and say “answer B.” Unlock and inspect the event log. The microphone may stop immediately on lock.
